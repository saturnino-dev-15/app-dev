# Inside Out 2 (2024)

## About the Movie
*Inside Out 2* is an animated film produced by **Pixar Animation Studios** and released by **Walt Disney Pictures**. The story continues the journey of Riley Andersen as she enters her teenage years and experiences new emotions that affect her thoughts, decisions, and relationships.

## Main Characters
- Joy
- Sadness
- Anger
- Fear
- Disgust
- Riley Andersen
- Anxiety
- Envy
- Embarrassment
- Ennui

## Why It Is My Favorite Movie
1. It has a meaningful and relatable story.
2. It teaches the importance of understanding emotions.
3. The characters are entertaining and memorable.
4. It shows the challenges of growing up and self-discovery.

### Personal Reflection
I enjoy *Inside Out 2* because it shows how emotions influence our decisions and relationships. The movie teaches that every emotion has a purpose and that accepting our feelings helps us grow as individuals.
